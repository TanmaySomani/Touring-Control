# Touring Control — Flight Inventory & Commercial Analytics Dashboard

[Live dashboard](https://touring-control-tanmaysomani.streamlit.app/) · [GitHub repository](https://github.com/TanmaySomani/Touring-Control)

- Built an interactive Python/Streamlit dashboard to monitor 48 simulated flight commitments, 1,632 contracted seats and 748 bookings, with filters, inventory reviews and reporting exports.
- Integrated 252 official BITRE observations across six international city pairs using a reproducible Python pipeline, SQLite reporting and booking-to-contract reconciliation.
- Compared seasonal and trend-adjusted demand forecasts using chronological validation and a separate holdout, achieving 6.0% weighted absolute percentage error on Jan–Jun 2026 market demand.
- Developed contract-constrained seat-release scenarios that protect confirmed passengers, minimum group sizes and safety buffers while evaluating capacity, fees and illustrative contribution.
- Created formula-driven Excel reports, CSV downloads, data-quality exception tracking and a business case supporting cross-functional inventory and commercial decision-making.

All commercial inventory and financial outcomes are simulated. Forecast accuracy relates to the official BITRE passenger data, not Flight Centre sales or bookings.
